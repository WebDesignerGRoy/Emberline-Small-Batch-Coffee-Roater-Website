Our story

Roasted with intention, chosen — never made.

We started Emberline in a 12-square-meter roaster in 2019 with a simple frustration: the cupping notes on throwaway bags described farms we could never find again. So we built relationships instead.

Today we work directly with 23 growers across 12 countries — paying 2.1× commodity prices, publishing every farm's altitude, wash process and roast date, and shipping before the coffee peaks and fades.

  • Direct-trade, always. No brokers, no mystery — every farm visits us annually to taste and approve.
  • Roast curves from real data. We log bean temperature, drop point and development time on every batch.
  • Freshness you can verify. Every bag carries its roast date and a cupping-notes QR code.
Direct from the farm

Options ship from Ethiopia, Colombia, Kenya and 9 more high-altitude regions — traceable to the farm gate.

Compostable valve bags, mushroom packaging for gear, and a returns program on any bag brewed within 14 days.
